Terms & Privacy Policy

Plain-language information about this beta app. This page describes the app's current implementation, not a promise of permanent availability or legal advice.

Important

DeftAI is an experimental service. Do not enter passwords for other services, payment details, government ID numbers, confidential documents, or other highly sensitive information. AI responses can be wrong; verify important information independently.

1. Terms of use

  • Use DeftAI lawfully and respectfully. Do not attempt to disrupt the service, access another user's account, abuse endpoints, or upload content you do not have permission to use.
  • You are responsible for reviewing AI-generated answers, code, files and research before relying on or publishing them.
  • DeftAI is provided on an experimental, best-effort basis. Features, models, limits and availability may change; chats or generated outputs may be unavailable or lost.
  • You retain responsibility for content you submit and for checking that your use of generated output follows applicable laws and third-party terms.
  • There is currently no in-app paid subscription, purchase flow, refund system or service-level guarantee.

2. Privacy & data collection

Information the app stores

  • Account details: username, password salt and a derived password hash. The app does not store the plain-text password in its account record.
  • Chats: conversation history saved to the account so it can be loaded again.
  • Preferences: personalization fields such as preferred name, response style, language and custom instructions.
  • Consent record: the time and policy version accepted when an account is created, plus the optional chat-review consent setting.
  • Security activity logs: signup events and successful/failed login metadata (username, event type and timestamp; no passwords or authentication tokens).
  • Optional chat-review logs: only if you turn on the separate “Allow DeftAI owner to review my chat messages and AI replies” setting in Personalization, DeftAI stores your text prompt and generated text reply in the owner activity panel. This is optional and can be switched off at any time; turning it off prevents new transcript logging but does not automatically erase earlier logs.

Information processed to provide features

  • Messages and relevant conversation context are sent to the configured AI provider (currently Groq) to generate responses. Images attached to a prompt may also be sent to the configured vision model.
  • When live research is triggered, search queries may be sent to the configured search provider (Tavily when configured, otherwise a DuckDuckGo HTML search fallback). Research results are restricted by the app to Reddit and selected official government domains.
  • If you ask DeftAI to read a website, the server may request that public URL and process its readable page content.
  • The hosting provider may process ordinary technical information such as request metadata and server logs under its own policies. The app also uses basic in-memory rate limiting.

Storage, security and retention

Activity logs are limited to 500 recent events and are pruned after 30 days when a new event is recorded. The owner can delete all activity logs from the admin panel. Optional transcript logs are not end-to-end encrypted and may be visible to the service owner. The current app stores its JSON data in /tmp/deftai-data.json on the hosting instance. This is temporary instance storage, not a durable database; data can be lost after a restart, redeploy or instance replacement. Do not rely on it as your only copy. Passwords are hashed, but this does not mean chats are end-to-end encrypted. The app does not promise a particular retention period, backup schedule or security certification.

Sharing and sale

Submitted content is processed by the AI/search providers needed to run the features you choose. This policy does not claim that those providers never retain or process request data; consult their current privacy terms. DeftAI does not intentionally display your private chat history to other users. No sale of personal data is part of the app's intended operation.

Your choices

  • Do not submit information you do not want processed by the app or its providers.
  • You can export the currently open conversation using the chat export control.
  • You can switch optional chat-review logging on or off in Settings → Personalization. Turning it off affects future messages; ask the service owner to delete previously collected log entries if needed.
  • There is not yet a self-service account-wide deletion control. Until one is implemented, do not use this beta for information that requires guaranteed deletion or retention controls.

3. Third-party services

DeftAI may rely on Groq for model inference, Tavily or DuckDuckGo for search, Render or another configured host for deployment, and public websites for research. Each service has its own terms, availability and privacy practices. DeftAI does not control those services.

4. Changes and contact

These policies may be updated as the app changes. The version and effective date are shown at the top of this page. For questions or corrections, use the project's public repository: DeftAI on GitHub.
