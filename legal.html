Terms & Privacy Policy

Plain-language information about this beta app. This page describes the app's current implementation, not a promise of permanent availability or legal advice.

Important

DeftAI is an experimental service. Do not enter passwords for other services, payment details, government ID numbers, confidential documents, or other highly sensitive information. AI responses can be wrong; verify important information independently.

1. Terms of use

  • Use DeftAI lawfully and respectfully. Do not attempt to disrupt the service, access another user's account, abuse endpoints, or upload content you do not have permission to use.
  • You are responsible for reviewing AI-generated answers, code, files and research before relying on or publishing them.
  • DeftAI is provided on an experimental, best-effort basis. Features, models, limits and availability may change; chats or generated outputs may be unavailable or lost.
  • You retain responsibility for content you submit and for checking that your use of generated output follows applicable laws and third-party terms.
  • Paid plan requests are handled manually: a user submits a request, the owner checks payment outside the app, and credits are added only after manual approval. DeftAI does not collect card or bank details or automatically verify a payment.

2. Privacy & data collection

Information the app stores

  • Account details: username, email address supplied at signup, password salt and a derived password hash. Signup does not require email verification. The app does not store the plain-text password in its account record.
  • AI chats: conversation history saved to the account so it can be loaded again.
  • Community data: public project posts, follow relationships, recent presence, and text-only direct-message threads. Direct messages may begin as requests; members can accept, block or report conversations. DM threads are not end-to-end encrypted, are stored by the service, and are limited to the most recent 500 messages per conversation. Do not send private or sensitive information in DMs. Reports include the reporter, reported member and reason and may be reviewed by the service owner.
  • Promo code records: code redemption and usage information used to enforce per-account and total-use limits.
  • Preferences: personalization fields such as preferred name, response style, language and custom instructions.
  • Consent record: the time and policy version accepted when an account is created, plus the optional chat-review consent setting.
  • Security activity logs: signup events and successful/failed login metadata (username, event type and timestamp; no passwords or authentication tokens).
  • Optional chat-review logs: only if you turn on the separate “Allow DeftAI owner to review my chat messages and AI replies” setting in Personalization, DeftAI stores your text prompt and generated text reply in the owner activity panel. This is optional and can be switched off at any time; turning it off prevents new transcript logging but does not automatically erase earlier logs.

Information processed to provide features

  • AI chat messages and relevant conversation context are sent to the selected, configured model provider (Groq, Google Gemini or OpenAI, depending on the server configuration) to generate responses. Images attached to an AI prompt may also be sent to the configured vision model. Community direct messages are stored separately and are not sent to the AI model unless a user separately copies them into an AI chat.
  • When live research is triggered, search queries may be sent to Tavily when configured or to a DuckDuckGo HTML search fallback. Results can come from a wider range of public websites; the assistant is instructed to prefer primary and reputable sources, but search results are not guaranteed to be complete or correct.
  • If you ask DeftAI to read a website, the server may request that public URL and process its readable page content.
  • Image generation requests may be sent to the configured image provider (currently Stability AI, if enabled). Promo codes may add credits or apply a discount to a manual plan request; no automatic payment is collected by the app.
  • The hosting provider may process ordinary technical information such as request metadata and server logs under its own policies. The app also uses basic in-memory rate limiting.

Storage, security and retention

Activity logs are limited to 500 recent events and are pruned after 30 days when a new event is recorded. The owner can delete all activity logs from the admin panel. Optional transcript logs are not end-to-end encrypted and may be visible to the service owner. The app stores JSON data at the path configured by DATA_DIR (default /tmp). On Render, this is temporary unless a persistent disk is mounted and DATA_DIR points to that disk. Data can be lost after a restart, redeploy or instance replacement when temporary storage is used. Do not rely on it as your only copy. Passwords are hashed, but this does not mean chats are end-to-end encrypted. The app does not promise a particular retention period, backup schedule or security certification.

Sharing and sale

AI chat content is processed by the AI/search providers needed to run the features you choose. This policy does not claim that those providers never retain or process request data; consult their current privacy terms. AI chat history is not displayed to other users. Community projects you publish are public to members, and direct messages are visible to the participants in the conversation; message reports and moderation metadata may be reviewed by the service owner. No sale of personal data is part of the app's intended operation.

Your choices

  • Do not submit information you do not want processed by the app or its providers.
  • You can export the currently open conversation using the chat export control.
  • You can switch optional chat-review logging on or off in Settings → Personalization. Turning it off affects future messages; ask the service owner to delete previously collected log entries if needed.
  • There is not yet a self-service account-wide deletion control. Until one is implemented, do not use this beta for information that requires guaranteed deletion or retention controls.

3. Third-party services

DeftAI may rely on Groq, Google Gemini or OpenAI for model inference; Stability AI for image generation when configured; Tavily or DuckDuckGo for live search; Render or another configured host for deployment; and public websites for research. The actual providers depend on server configuration and enabled features. Each service has its own terms, availability and privacy practices. DeftAI does not control those services.

4. Changes and contact

These policies may be updated as the app changes. The version and effective date are shown at the top of this page. For questions or corrections, use the project's public repository: DeftAI on GitHub.
